Chat rules

The chat is open to read for everyone. To write, you need a free account (18+ only, like the rest of Snapline). Your username and favorite team are shown next to your messages.

  • Be respectful. No insults, harassment, threats, hate or discrimination (origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability…), no sexual content.
  • No spam or advertising, no links to other sites, no betting tips for real-money gambling.
  • Keep personal data private — yours and other people's: no real names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses.
  • Don't impersonate Snapline staff or another person. Messages from the Snapline team carry a Snapline badge.
  • Mind the spoilers: other people may be watching a replay or a game that started earlier.
  • Some words are hidden automatically, and messages with links, contact details or hate are not sent. To keep the chat readable, you can send one message every 3 seconds, 10 per minute.

Reporting a message

Use Report on any message (spam, abuse, hate, spoiler, other), or write to the contact below. A message reported by several people is hidden until we review it. We review reports as quickly as we can and act on content that breaks these rules or the law.

What we can do, and how you are told

We may hide a message, or stop an account from chatting for a time or for good. When we do, the person concerned sees it in the chat with the reason (the rule broken). If you disagree with a decision, write to the contact below and we will look at it again. You can mute someone for yourself at any time (Mute on their message): only you stop seeing their messages.

Your messages

You can delete your own messages at any time (Delete on your message). Messages are deleted automatically after __DAYS__ days. You keep the rights to what you write; by posting, you let Snapline show it in the chat for that time.

  • Chat (accounts only, if you write): your messages, with your username, favorite team, the room and the time; reports you make (which message, the reason); moderation decisions about your account and their reason. Messages are deleted after __DAYS__ days, reports and decisions after 6 months. Your IP address is never stored with a message.
